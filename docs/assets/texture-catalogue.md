# ChronoGenesis: texture catalogue

Every texture in the mod, under `src/main/resources/assets/chronogenesis/textures/` in the repo (branch `feature/early-game`).
All of them are generated by `tools/textures.py`; edit that script and re-run it rather than painting over PNGs.

**Regenerate:** `python3 tools/textures.py src/main/resources/assets/chronogenesis/textures [preview.png]`  
**Preview models:** `python3 tools/render_models.py out.png` (after pulling the datagen commit)

Shading: every texture goes through the finishing pass in `tools/textures.py` (`finish()`): an edge-preserving smooth, then each material region recoloured from a five-tone, hue-shifted ramp (cool shadows, warm highlights) lit from the top-left: form shadow and bevels on sprites, a soft tiling relief on blocks. Tint masks, glass and model sheets are left out (model sheets are only smoothed).

Art direction: Frankenstein (stitches, staples, electrodes, jars) + Call of Cthulhu (eyes, maws, tentacles, eldritch green) + steampunk (brass, copper, rivets, valves). Frames use the Block of Brass texture.

Layering: containers = body + `_fill` (tinted by aspect) + `_front`; culture = growth (tinted) + dish; flesh and core = flesh (tinted) + overlay.

## Models

The generated block states and models live in `src/generated/resources/assets/chronogenesis/` (datagen output; change `ModBlockStateProvider` / `ModItemModelProvider`, not the JSON). Organism models are built in `ModBlockStateProvider` from shared parts: the brass frame (12 beams), tinted flesh masses, untinted brass and eyes. Flesh has `growth` 0-3 (tendrils, creeping, swelling, mature); cores have `phase` (seed, awake, hungry, necrotic); grafts are modelled pointing up and rotated by facing; the Brass Conduit is a multipart (core + arms, bare / vein / clogged).
