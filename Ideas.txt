I thought more along the line of this: the essence are physical (almost philosophical) attributes: A mobs gravity/density/weights, a mobs conciousness/awareness, a mobs speed, a mobs size, a mobs time (the temporal essence). Different needles can extract different aspects, and not every aspect can be stored in a simple syringe, leading to big essence farms having to be built, which utilise grown machines, which are fed with precicely the essence one wants to farm, leading to the machine hungering for more of that essence, and expanding into the frame provided. Essence is converted into growth, and if there is no space left, the essence is stored in the machine (up to its capacity). Grafting different flesh parts onto the machine will result in different things. One graftable attachement e.g. is an output, which taps into the stored essence and makes it accessible to the player (or other machines). It can be used to move the essence into glass vials (or jugs which are placeable). Some machines are there for farming purposes, some are meant to instill powers into the player. Using the stage one Enhancing Gland will fill the player with essence (functioning as mana). Different essences grant different powers. Level 1 powers are: Density Essence: The players fists become as dense as iron, allowing them to mine anything an iron pick could mine (and drop the items), but at the cost of being as slow as a stone pick. Mobs who have their density removed start floating a set distance from the floor, if more is removed then they start rising further and further (only a leash can keep them grounded), Awareness Essence: the more a mobs awareness essence is removed, the slower its AI will update, until it wont tick at all (if all essence is removed). The players Level 1 awareness allows them to see mobs and non-sneaking players in a 20 block radius with the glowing effect. Speed and size are self explanatory and scale with the Level of the power. Time is the most important one and what this mod is centered around. Time essence is the hardest to farm, as the machines to store it are highly dangerous and unstable. Only small amounts are farmable at the beginning, and only from different mobs. Too much farming will cause Time Tears. To get enough time essence at the beginning of the game, the other essences have to be combined in such a way, as to satisfy the physical formuals. These should be relatively simple, and roughly proportional to the real world equivalents e.g. Speed = Distance/Time   The process of getting time essence from speed essence and utilising distance in it (e.g. having speed essence be transported through a long pipe (or something, come up with a clever way to do this) should feel like a little puzzle. The wording of the formulas in the books must be understandable in such a way, that moving around parts is not really maths, but more combining different things. Higher level temporal powers allow the player to slow down time in a given area, speed up time in a given area, and set an entire chunk into a time loop. Each day the chunk is reset to the state it was when it was set into the time loop. This allows for duplication of natural resources. Chests and other containers will not have their stuff reset, come up with a reasonable in-world reason why that cannot happen (as this would just allow duping). Stopping time in a chunk should also make the daytime in that chunk be different than the other chunks (this also goes for slowed or sped up chunks). Until the player researches and crafts the essence transfer bauble (that they can equip) which connects to an essence storage, they cannot regenerate their essence outside of the gland. Also to normalise the player, they have to craft equipment, that protects against excess temporal essence. Research is done via the Temporal Control Unit (or any other magical/technological name for the bio computer). It acts as an interface for essence flow/control/access, research (which requires essence and other materials), and later temporal shifts (the time travel aspects). To stabilise the farm animals used for essence farming, temporal fields have to be set up, as to allow the animals to regenerate their lost essence. Without these, animals have to be bred to then farm the adults, and then later do the same with the new adults. Another use for temporal essence is the Temporal Lense, a device that uses temporal essence stored in it (or the player) to peer into the past (a hazy version of the world seed, but altered (to look more like the past). The power setting indicates how far back the player can see (and thus how much changed the world is). New structures are part of world generation, abandoned labs, other abandoned houses or areas (all with a purpose), ruins etc. The temporal lense can see (depending how far back it can see) the full castle instead of the ruin, the bustling marketplace instead of an abandoned village, and using hints seen there (all tracked in a journal) to further research. Blueprints for machines can be found in labs, but rarely in abandoned ones. Scanning a laid out blueprint through the temporal lense will add a scribbled version into the journal. These can be used in research to further the goal of temporal mastery. Also shrines and obelisks can be found. these seem to be almost unaffected by time, and will distort the temporal lense view around them (like a black  hole with an even horizon).
